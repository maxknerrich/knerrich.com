# Offer cards

The end of the landing page: "Work with me", two ways to start, building with agents and design engineering, under a green glow that fades in from above and reaches the window's edges.

- Side by side on wide screens, stacked on phones.
- The agents card lists the four parts of the work in a line each: feedback loops, environments, validation, coaching. The design card lists what I design and build.
- One card is consulting for teams that want to work with agents, the other is designing and building a product for you. Each is one link and one tab stop: an email with its own subject. Hovering or focusing it lights its edge and rolls "Contact me" to the address.
- A round holo sticker with the offer's pixel icon sits over each card's top-right corner. It slaps on after the card rises in; while the card is hovered or focused its foil runs through its colours and its glitter twinkles.
- With reduced motion the stickers are there from the start and stay still.
- A sticker never covers the title, down to 360px.
