# Stickers

Stickers on the landing page to peel off, carry and stick anywhere, by mouse or touch.

- Pulling an edge part way peels it and lets it spring back; all the way lifts it to carry.
- On touch, dragging a sticker doesn't scroll the page.
- They start in fixed spots that never cover text, and fall in on load.
- Where they are survives a reload.
- They make sounds and stay out of the way of keyboards and screen readers.
