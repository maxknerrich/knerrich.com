# Porsche Driver Experience

- UX design
- Automotive HMI
- Infotainment

Infotainment interface design for the Porsche Macan and Cayenne, as part of the Driver Experience team.

![Cayenne interior with its central and passenger infotainment displays](/projects/porsche/cayenne.jpg)

During my time at Porsche, I worked on a redesign of the infotainment experience.

## Macan

My work included the Drive screen, navigation, and shared system elements. The Drive screen brings vehicle settings and driving modes together, with a vehicle visualisation alongside the controls.

![Macan infotainment display showing the Drive screen and drive mode selection](/projects/porsche/drive.jpg)

*Macan Drive screen.*

## Cayenne

For the Cayenne, I also worked on the Drive screen, charging, and widgets. This extended my work across vehicle controls and the other information available through the infotainment system.
