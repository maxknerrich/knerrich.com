# Annex Tracker

- Product design
- UX research
- Prototyping
- Design system

Product and UX design for a legal tech tool that helps M&A teams create and maintain annex lists.

I was the only designer on the team, responsible for research, user flows, prototypes, and the design system. The interface brings document status, responsibilities, and related correspondence into one tracker.

I started by looking at competing tools and the structure of existing annex lists, then used early prototypes to explore the user flow. The work covered both the application and the documents people needed to take out of it.

![Annex Tracker interface with an annex list and a document details panel](/projects/annextracker/tracker.jpg)

## Beyond the interface

PDF exports were a major part of the work. Clients needed readable, company-branded documents, so I designed the exported lists alongside summary pages and comparisons between versions.

![An exported annex list showing references, titles, and updates](/projects/annextracker/export.jpg)

Alongside the product screens, I built a Figma design system for high-fidelity prototypes. A restrained visual style kept the focus on dense tables, document details, and status information.
