# Video

A short muted clip that loops in place of an image, as wide as the page's images.

- It plays only while at least half of it is on screen, and pauses when scrolled away.
- With reduced motion it waits on its poster until the reader presses play.
- A pause button sits on the clip, reachable by keyboard; screen readers hear the clip's description and the button's state.
- Without JavaScript it shows its poster with the browser's own controls.
- The poster is the clip's first frame, so nothing jumps when it starts.
